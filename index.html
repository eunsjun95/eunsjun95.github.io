Research

I use single-molecule fluorescence to watch how nucleosomes open and close, and how transcription factors, histone modifications and a cancer fusion protein change that motion.

May 2023 – now

Ph.D. research

Supervisor: Prof. Michael Poirier, The Ohio State University

  • Single-molecule measurements of nucleosome kinetics in the presence of a transcription factor, with and without the SAGA and ATAC histone acetyltransferase (HAT) modules.
  • Effects of post-translational modifications (PTMs) on nucleosome kinetics in response to transcription factor binding, measured by TIRF microscopy.
  • Nucleosomal DNA unwrapping and rewrapping rates in response to the cancer fusion protein EWS::FLI.
